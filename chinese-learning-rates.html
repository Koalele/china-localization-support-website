FAQ

Who are the teachers?

Mandarin teachers who hold the International Chinese Language Teacher Certificate (CTCSOL) and have experience teaching adults online.

Do I need to know any Mandarin?

No. We work with complete beginners as well as learners who want to improve.

Is the learning plan really free?

Yes. The consultation is free and there is no obligation to book lessons.

How do I pay?

By international bank transfer or Wise. If you are in China, you can also pay with Alipay or WeChat Pay. All prices are settled in RMB.

Ready to start?

Tell us your goals and we will send you a free learning plan within 1 business day.
