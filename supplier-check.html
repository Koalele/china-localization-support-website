How it works

  1. 01

    Send the details

    The supplier’s name, website or contact, the product and what you want to check.

  2. 02

    We check

    We search the public records in Chinese and, if you choose, call the supplier.

  3. 03

    You receive the summary

    A one-page summary in English, with the sources we checked.

What this check is, and what it is not

The check reports what public records show and what the supplier tells us. It is not a due diligence report, a credit rating or a legal opinion, and it does not guarantee how the supplier will perform.

For legal or financial checks, we can introduce licensed firms.
