Wuhan AI & Chinese Culture Immersion (working title)

Program in development

A short-stay program for international students who want to see where artificial intelligence is heading, and experience China's culture and language while they do. Set in Wuhan, one of China's leading university cities.

Join the Early Access List

Program concept (illustrative, subject to confirmation)

AI & Emerging Technology

  • Explore how AI sees, learns, reasons and creates, from computer vision and machine learning to large language models and generative AI.
  • Discussion of AI's role in industry and society.

Learning & Discussion

  • Sessions and talks led by university academics and AI researchers (planned).
  • Small-group challenges and mentoring.

Chinese Language & Culture

  • Chinese for everyday and academic settings.
  • Cultural activities and local visits.

Illustrative outline

  1. AI foundations and everyday applications
  2. How machines see and learn: vision and machine learning
  3. Large language models and generative AI: how they work, and what they can and cannot do
  4. Intelligent systems and robotics in the real world
  5. AI, ethics and society: a student debate
  6. Chinese for science and technology conversations
  7. Wuhan cultural experiences and visits to local innovation sites

Planned for international students aged 16 and above. Applicants under 18 will need a parent or guardian to register and consent.

Dates, venue, fees and academic partners are still being finalized and will be confirmed before registration opens. We do not take payment at this stage.

Registering your interest is free and carries no obligation. We will contact you when program details are confirmed, and let you know if the program does not go ahead.
