FAQ

Do students need a visa?

Citizens of many countries, including France, Japan, Australia and New Zealand, can currently enter China visa-free for up to 30 days for exchange visits. Others need a study visa, for which the host university provides an admission notice. Please confirm with the Chinese embassy in your country.

How are students under 18 supervised?

Teachers travel with school groups, and a bilingual coordinator stays with the group throughout. A parent or guardian registers and consents for every student under 18, and every program has insurance and a safety plan.

How much does a program cost?

Each program is quoted per group, based on length, group size and content. Ask us for a sample quote for your group.
