Wuhan Breakfast Walk in Old Hankou

Wuhan locals call breakfast “guozao”, and the city takes it seriously. Your host takes you to neighbourhood stalls for hot dry noodles, doupi and soup dumplings, then walks you through the old concession streets of Hankou.
