FAQ

Who leads the experience?

Experiences are led by local hosts and specialists, and, where guiding is involved, by licensed guides, all coordinated by us.

Can experiences be held in English, French or Japanese?

Yes, tell us your preferred language.

What is not included?

Transport, hotels, meals and entrance tickets are not included unless listed. Tell us if you need them and we will help arrange them.

Can you arrange other cities?

We can plan experiences in other destinations on request. Availability depends on local providers and is confirmed in your proposal.

Are experiences suitable for children?

Families are welcome when children are accompanied by their parents or guardians. We do not currently organize programs for unaccompanied minors.
