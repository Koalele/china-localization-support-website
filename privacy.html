Privacy Policy

How TransWind handles information submitted through this website.

Who we are

This website is operated by 武汉渡风文化传播有限公司 (“TransWind”, “we”), based in Wuhan, China. You can reach us at hello@transwindcn.com.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry through our contact or program forms, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organization, location, the service you are interested in, timing, budget range and your message. Program enquiries may also ask for a participant’s age range and a parent or guardian’s contact details.

We do not ask for passport, ID or payment card numbers through the website. Please do not include them, or other sensitive or confidential information, in a form. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages. With your consent, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site. We do not use advertising trackers.

Analytics and cookies

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics, a service provided by Google, sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, how you arrived at the site, your approximate location and your device type. We look at this information only in aggregate, to improve the site. Google processes it under its own Privacy Policy. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded.

You can change your choice at any time: Cookie settings.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, plan and deliver services, and keep a record of our communications. We do not sell personal information.

Who receives it

Form submissions are transmitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form service operated by Web3Creative, and forwarded to our email. Web3Forms and our email provider process information under their own privacy notices and terms; see the Web3Forms Privacy Policy and Terms. Where a project requires it, we share only the information needed with the translators, interpreters or local partners involved.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond, deliver services and meet legal or accounting requirements, then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have sent us, or ask any question about this policy, by emailing hello@transwindcn.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children and young people

This website is intended for adults. Enquiries about programs for participants under 18 should be submitted by a parent, guardian or school representative.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
