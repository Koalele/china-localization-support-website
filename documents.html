Documents by type

Translation is available in any combination of Chinese, English, Japanese and French.

Personal & civil documents

Passports, ID cards, household registers (hukou), birth, marriage and divorce certificates, death certificates and name-change records.

Typical delivery: 1–2 business days

Suggested level: Standard

Immigration & visa materials

Police clearance certificates, employment and income letters, proof of relationship, bank statements, and asset and property proofs.

For Canadian immigration (IRCC), we can arrange the translator’s sworn affidavit. Read our guide

Typical delivery: 2–3 business days

Suggested level: Standard or Reviewed

Academic records

Diplomas, degree certificates, transcripts, enrollment letters and recommendation letters.

Typical delivery: 1–2 business days

Suggested level: Standard

Business & legal documents

Business licenses, articles of association, contracts, powers of attorney, court filings and judgments, financial and audit reports.

Typical delivery: quoted per project

Suggested level: Reviewed or Senior

Driving & vehicle documents

Driver's licenses and vehicle registration certificates.

Typical delivery: 1 business day

Suggested level: Standard

Delivery times are typical for standard-length documents. Exact timing is confirmed in your quote, and urgent requests can be discussed.
