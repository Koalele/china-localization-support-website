Sino-Canadian Youth Leadership Camp

In July 2024, we co-ran a Sino-Canadian youth leadership summer camp in two seven-day residential sessions.

July 2024, two sessions
Seven days, residential
Participants from across China
Canadian teachers, entrepreneurs and student mentors

Campers lived, ate and worked together with Canadian mentors in an English-speaking setting. Each day combined project-based workshops, team challenges, outdoor activities, Canadian culture sessions and a Chinese culture exchange, so campers practised English and leadership skills through doing rather than studying.

Mentors included Canadian ESL teachers, business owners and young Canadian student mentors working alongside Chinese team leaders.

A typical day

  • MorningIcebreakers and project workshops
  • AfternoonTeam challenges, Canadian sports, Chinese calligraphy and handicrafts
  • EveningTalent show, group games and reflection

Delivered with Canadian partners. Photos are cropped to protect participant privacy.
