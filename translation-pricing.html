Which level do I need?

Standard

Translated by a vetted university-trained translator and checked by our team. Suited to personal, civil and academic documents and everyday business papers.

Reviewed

A standard translation followed by a second full review by another linguist. Suited to contracts, immigration and visa files and company materials.

Senior

Translated and signed off by an experienced professional with subject knowledge. Suited to legal, financial and technical texts, and anything going to print.

Payment and orders

How can I pay from outside China?

By international bank transfer or Wise. If you are in China, you can also pay with Alipay or WeChat Pay. All prices are settled in RMB.

Do I pay before work starts?

Smaller orders are paid in full before we start. Larger projects need a 50% deposit, with the balance due on delivery. Your quote states which applies.

Can you certify translations?

Yes. Certified translations carry TransWind’s company translation seal and a signed statement that the translation is accurate and complete. Requirements differ between authorities, so please check what yours accepts before you order.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Revisions are free within 7 days of delivery, whether they fix an error or reflect your feedback on the translation.

Are my documents kept confidential?

Yes. Every translator has signed a confidentiality agreement, and your files are used only for your project.

How long does a translation take?

Most personal and academic documents are delivered in 1–2 business days. Larger or specialized files are scheduled in your quote, and urgent requests can be arranged.
