What makes it different

Meeting notes the same day

Bilingual notes with names, prices, quantities, lead times and agreed next steps, sent the same evening.

Prepared before you arrive

A short briefing on your products and goals, so your interpreter arrives with the right terminology.

How the day works

  1. 01

    Before

    Tell us who you are meeting and what you want to achieve. We confirm the schedule and prepare the briefing.

  2. 02

    On the day

    Your interpreter joins you for meetings, factory tours and meals within the booked hours.

  3. 03

    That evening

    You receive bilingual meeting notes for each meeting.

  4. 04

    Within 3 days

    We translate your follow-up messages, so suppliers receive a clear reply in Chinese.
